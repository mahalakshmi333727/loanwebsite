Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Renoahbusinesshub website uses cookies and similar technologies, and what choices you have. It describes the website as it is built today.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third-party service can store on your browser or device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. They are commonly used to make websites work, remember preferences or measure usage.

Cookies set by this website

The code of this website does not set its own cookies and does not store information in your browser's local storage or session storage. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website, and we do not use advertising pixels or remarketing tags.

Because no non-essential cookies are set by us, this website does not show a cookie consent banner. If we add analytics or marketing tools in the future, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where required.

How you can control cookies

  • Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies in their settings. You can usually block third-party cookies only, or clear cookies each time you close the browser.
  • Private or incognito windows discard cookies when you close them.
  • If you block cookies from third parties, the website should still work, although the embedded map may not display fully.
